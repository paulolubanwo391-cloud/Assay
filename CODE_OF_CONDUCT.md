# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Assay exists to test claims with evidence. That only works if people can disagree about numbers without it becoming personal. Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, is expected to help keep it a place where that is possible, whatever their background, experience, identity or position on NEAR.

## Expected behaviour

- Argue with data, methods and reasoning. Say what you measured and how.
- Assume a mistake before assuming bad faith, and say so plainly when you find one.
- Accept correction. A reproducible discrepancy is a contribution, not an attack.
- Be clear about what you know, what you assume and what you could not measure.
- Give credit for other people's work, including work outside this repository.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Presenting fabricated or selectively edited data as measurement.
- Promoting a token, product or trade in project spaces, or presenting any content here as financial advice.
- Sustained disruption of discussions after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and wherever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainers through the repository's private vulnerability reporting channel (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability"), marking it as a conduct report, or by contacting a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Say what happened, where, and when, with links if you have them. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review every report and respond in proportion to what happened:

1. **Correction.** A private note explaining what was wrong and what is expected.
2. **Warning.** A formal warning, with consequences stated for a repeat.
3. **Temporary ban** from interacting with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may be removed from their role.
